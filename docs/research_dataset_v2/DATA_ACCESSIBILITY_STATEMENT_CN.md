# Data Accessibility Statement 模板

更新时间：2026-03-16  
适用范围：论文、项目报告、附录、数据说明页。

---

## 1. 推荐写法（英文短版，可直接放论文）

### Version A: hybrid open + restricted sources

Data used in this study were assembled from a mixture of openly accessible and restricted-access audio sources.  
Openly accessible sources are cited individually in the manuscript and project documentation.  
Some additional audio materials were accessed under non-commercial academic research terms, institutional archive conditions, gated dataset approval, or project-specific permission. Due to copyright and redistribution restrictions, we do not redistribute those raw audio files.  
To support transparency and partial reproducibility, we release source metadata where permitted, data-processing scripts, dataset manifests, and experiment configurations. Access conditions for restricted sources are documented in the project data notes.

### Version B: mostly restricted research use

Some of the audio materials used in this study are not publicly redistributable. These materials were accessed lawfully for non-commercial academic research through archive access, dataset gating, or project-specific permission. We therefore do not release the raw audio. Instead, we provide metadata where permitted, processing scripts, and documentation describing the source categories, access conditions, and experimental pipeline.

## 2. 推荐写法（中文说明版）

本研究使用的音频数据由两部分组成：

- 可公开访问的开放音频源
- 受访问限制但可合法用于非商业学术研究的音频源

对于开放音频源，我们在论文和项目文档中逐项给出来源。  
对于受限音频源，我们仅在合法研究使用范围内访问原始音频，不对外再分发原始文件。  
为保证透明度与尽可能的可复现性，我们将公开以下材料（在许可允许范围内）：

- 数据来源说明
- 元数据与统计信息
- 数据处理与导入脚本
- 数据集 manifest
- 实验配置与评测脚本

对于受限源，其访问方式、限制条件和不再分发的原因将在项目文档中单独说明。

## 3. 适合当前项目的定制版

### 英文版

The cross-cultural music data used in this project combine openly accessible audio sources and restricted-access research materials. Openly accessible sources are cited directly in the paper and project documentation. Additional materials may be accessed through archive-based listening access, gated dataset approval, or project-specific academic permission. We do not redistribute raw audio files from restricted sources. Instead, we document source provenance, access conditions, metadata fields, and the full preprocessing and embedding pipeline so that the experimental setup remains transparent even when some raw media cannot be shared.

### 中文版

本项目的跨文化音乐数据同时包含开放音频源与受限研究访问音频源。对于开放音频源，论文与项目文档将直接给出来源信息；对于受限音频源，数据仅在非商业学术研究范围内合法访问，不再对外分发原始音频。为保证实验透明度，我们将公开来源说明、访问条件、元数据字段、预处理脚本、embedding 构建流程与实验配置，以便在原始音频不可公开的情况下仍尽可能保留可解释性与部分可复现性。
